Currency: prices are quoted in AMD exactly as the source shows them. ## Key question 1 — How much does a Yerevan customer pay for an at-home sofa / armchair / chair / mattress / carpet / car-interior cleaning? (price table, one row per provider)

### Takeaway
Published 2025–2026 Yerevan prices cluster at 5,000–8,000 AMD ($14–22) for a two-seat sofa, 7,000–14,000 AMD ($19–38) for a three-seat sofa, 12,000–30,000 AMD ($33–82) for a corner sofa, 3,000–6,000 AMD ($8–16) for an armchair, 500–3,500 AMD ($1.4–10) for a dining chair, 6,000–10,000 AMD ($16–27) for a mattress and 600–1,200 AMD/m² ($1.6–3.3) for carpet; private masters on list.am sit at the bottom of each range, branded companies (RT Cleaning, Clean Land, Clean House Service) at the top. "From" prices published by companies for any sofa are 8,000–12,000 AMD ($22–33).

### Inferences
- A realistic revenue-per-job model for a solo mobile operator in 2026: 2-seat sofa 6,000–8,000 AMD ($16–22), 3-seat 8,000–12,000 ($22–33), corner 12,000–18,000 ($33–49), armchair 3,000–5,000 ($8–14), dining chair 1,000–2,000 ($2.7–5.5), mattress 6,000–10,000 ($16–27), carpet 900–1,200/m² ($2.5–3.3). This sits between the list.am private-master floor (#22863082) and the RT Cleaning / Clean Land company ceiling.
- The most common household order (one sofa + one or two armchairs) lands at 11,000–15,000 AMD ($30–41) — the exact bundle Refresh Cleaning Service prices at 11,000 promo / 15,000 list.
- Corner sofas are the highest-value single item (12,000–30,000 AMD); Radiance's wide range suggests pricing by number of sections/seats on site rather than a fixed rate.
- Car-interior cleaning appears to be a separate segment (detailing/washes, rally.am car category) rather than a line item of furniture cleaners; only a couple of list.am ads offer it mobile.

### Gaps
- No provider page could be read directly; itemised prices for Cleantastic, YVN Clean, Cleanline, SV Cleaning, Elita, Devid, El Cleaning and CleaningYerevan were not present in any snippet — these appear to quote on request.
- Posting/renewal dates of list.am ads #22863082 and #22891545 were not captured (search quota exhausted before the follow-up).
- No Yerevan source with an explicit on-site car-interior price list was captured; the "from 10,000 AMD" figure is a category-level snippet.
- The Level up itemised list (turn.am) was only summarised as a 500–12,000 AMD range.

### Inferences
- The Russian-language cluster (RT Cleaning, Cleantastic, Helpers City, Clean Land, YVN Clean) is aimed at the post-2022 relocant segment and prices at the top of the market (8,000–12,000 AMD entry); the Armenian-language cluster (list.am, turn.am, Facebook) is 20–40% cheaper and reached mostly through classifieds and word of mouth; English pages are rare, so expat/diaspora customers searching in English find only Cleanline, Radiance, CleaningYerevan and turn.am.
- Nobody in the captured set markets fast drying ("сухо через 2 часа") or a written guarantee document; those are open differentiators, along with per-item transparent price lists (only RT Cleaning, Radiance, Clean Land and two list.am ads publish full lists).
- A solo operator posting trilingual ads (HY title + RU/EN keywords) on list.am, turn.am and Instagram would cover the same discovery channels the incumbents use, since none of them relies on Google Ads or a strong review base.
